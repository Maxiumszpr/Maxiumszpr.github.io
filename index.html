About Me

I am an undergraduate student in a double bachelor degree program, studying Computer Science at Tianjin University of Technology and Data Science and Business Intelligence at the University of Quebec. My research focuses on efficient and robust machine learning for safety-critical perception, industrial monitoring, and root cause analysis of failures in agentic systems.

My previous work spans civil infrastructure crack segmentation, RGB-X multimodal fusion, wind power forecasting, wind turbine fault diagnosis, log anomaly detection, SCADA-based time-series modeling, and root cause analysis of failures in agentic systems. I am particularly interested in efficient and robust representation learning, lightweight architectures, state space models, multimodal and multi-scale feature interaction, and reliable diagnosis of complex AI and industrial systems.
