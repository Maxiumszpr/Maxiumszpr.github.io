About Me

I am an undergraduate student in a double bachelor degree program, studying Computer Science at Tianjin University of Technology and Business Intelligence at the University of Quebec. My research focuses on efficient and robust machine learning for safety-critical perception and industrial monitoring.

My recent work spans civil infrastructure crack segmentation, RGB-X multimodal fusion, wind power forecasting, wind turbine fault diagnosis, log anomaly detection, and SCADA-based time-series modeling. I am especially interested in lightweight architectures, state space models, frequency-aware representation learning, multi-scale feature interaction, and topology-preserving segmentation.

Research Interests

Crack Segmentation

Efficient, structure-aware, and topology-preserving models for thin and low-contrast cracks.

RGB-X Fusion

Reliable multimodal perception with RGB, depth, thermal, and polarization information.

Time-Series Modeling

Lightweight spatio-temporal learning for wind power forecasting and SCADA monitoring.

Anomaly Detection

Frequency-aware representations for noisy, data-scarce, semi-structured event streams.
